seamm_optimizer.coordinates module#

Coordinate systems for the optimizer: Cartesians, and internal coordinates built with geomeTRIC’s code (TRIC, delocalized internals, redundant primitives), per the design (proposal §4, Paul’s §10 decision 1).

Every system works in atomic units internally (bohr, hartree) and offers:

  • q(xyz): the coordinates of a Cartesian structure (Å in, a vector out);

  • gradient(xyz, gx): the gradient in these coordinates (gx in eV/Å);

  • hessian(xyz, gx, hx): a Cartesian Hessian (eV/Å^2) in these coordinates;

  • to_cartesian(xyz, dq): the Cartesian structure for a step dq, and whether the back-transformation reproduced dq (the check of §4: a failure lets the optimizer shrink the step or fall back to a Cartesian step);

  • difference(xyz1, xyz0): q(xyz1) - q(xyz0), with angles wrapped;

  • rigid(xyz): an orthonormal basis of the coordinate changes of an overall translation or rotation, removed from every step.

seamm_optimizer.coordinates.BACK_TRANSFORM_TOLERANCE = 0.1#

the back-transformation is accepted when |dq_achieved - dq| <= this x |dq| (+1e-6)

seamm_optimizer.model_hessian module#

Model Hessians: cheap starting guesses for the optimizer.

lindh_hessian: R. Lindh, A. Bernhardsson, G. Karlström, P.-Å. Malmqvist, Chem. Phys. Lett. 241, 423 (1995). Force constants for every stretch, bend and torsion, each weighted by distance-dependent factors

rho_ij = exp(alpha_ij (r_ref,ij^2 - r_ij^2))

so that no bonding pattern is needed: bonded atoms get rho ~ 1, distant ones ~ 0. Parameters by periodic-table row (1: H-He, 2: Li-Ne, 3: Na and beyond), in atomic units. The Hessian is returned in Cartesians, eV/Å^2.

seamm_optimizer.model_hessian.RHO_CUTOFF = 0.0001#

products of rho smaller than this are left out

seamm_optimizer.optimizer module#

The optimizer: quasi-Newton minimization with an RFO / trust-region step, in Cartesians or internal coordinates (geomeTRIC’s TRIC, DLC, HDLC or primitives), a model or computed starting Hessian, BFGS updates in the coordinates used, a checked back-transformation with a Cartesian fallback, and a check that the end point is a minimum, with an escape from a saddle point.

The engine is any callable engine(xyz) -> (energy, gradient) with xyz an N x 3 array in Å, the energy in eV and the gradient N x 3 in eV/Å. Two Hessian engines, each (xyz) -> 3N x 3N in eV/Å^2, are optional:

  • hessian_engine, at the expensive level: the starting Hessian with hessian=”computed”, and the minimum check;

  • cheap_hessian_engine, at a cheap level (PM7, GFN2-xTB, an MLFF, a force field): the starting Hessian with hessian=”cheap”, refreshed when the quadratic model keeps predicting the energy change badly (refresh), and the minimum check when there is no expensive one (proposal §5).

Convergence is the shared criterion of the benchmark: the largest force on any atom at most fmax (default 0.0015 eV/Å, the CBH criterion).

seamm_optimizer.optimizer.ASE_IDENTITY = 70.0#

ASE BFGS’s starting Hessian, a scaled identity, kept for comparisons

seamm_optimizer.steps module#

Steps and Hessian updates for minimization.

rfo_step: the rational-function (RFO) step (Banerjee, Adams, Simons, Shepard, J. Phys. Chem. 89, 52 (1985)), and when it is longer than the trust radius, the restricted step of that length (the trust-region subproblem, solved for the level shift by Newton iterations on 1/|s|). Both are taken in the space orthogonal to null (overall translation and rotation for Cartesians).

bfgs_update: the BFGS update, skipped when the curvature condition fails, so the Hessian stays positive definite for minimization.

seamm_optimizer.steps.HESSIAN_FLOOR = 0.005#

the eigenvalue floor (atomic units) for computed Hessians used in minimization

seamm_optimizer.steps.bfgs_update(H, s, y, null=None)[source]#

The BFGS update of H with step s and gradient change y; unchanged (and False returned as the second value) when s.y is not clearly positive.

seamm_optimizer.steps.positive_hessian(H, floor=0.005, zero=1e-06)[source]#

A computed Hessian made fit for minimization: eigenvalues below floor are raised to it, except the near-zero ones (|w| < zero, the rigid-body directions, which the steps project out). A Hessian computed away from its own minimum has negative eigenvalues; clamped, a UFF Hessian on the MMFF proxy took 352 gradients over 25 CHNO PM7 starts against 504 raw (abs values: 418; Design 2026-11-24).

Module contents#

seamm_optimizer: robust geometry optimization for SEAMM.

Phase 1 (proposal OPTIMIZER_DESIGN_PROPOSAL_20261124): quasi-Newton minimization in Cartesians with an RFO / trust-region step, Lindh’s model Hessian or a computed one, BFGS updates, and a minimum check with an escape from saddle points.
